# Clustering

Keep different notions of similarity explicit. A future workflow may compare
recordings by melody, arrangement, lyrics, rhythm, performance, or cultural
context, then expose those dimensions and their evidence in its results.

Important distinctions to preserve include:

- same composition versus similar-sounding recording;
- cover, translation, adaptation, and medley relationships;
- acoustic similarity versus shared lyrical meaning;
- model-inferred relationships versus user-confirmed links.

Do not commit a universal similarity score or confidence threshold before
evaluating it on a representative, multilingual collection. Treat Telugu,
Tamil, Hindi, English, Carnatic repertoire, and other traditions as evaluation
strata rather than assuming one language or genre represents all use.
